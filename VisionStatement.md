# ✨ Vision Statement
Empowering lifelong learners with effortless, daily word recall one notification at a time.

## 🧭 Purpose of the Project
To help users internalize vocabulary, concepts, and foreign language words through a simple and consistent daily notification system — turning passive moments into learning opportunities.

## 🎯 Target Audience
Students preparing for exams

Language learners at any level

Curious individuals who want to expand their vocabulary

Busy professionals who want to keep learning in a low-effort, high-impact way

## 💡 Core Values
Curiosity: Encouraging a natural love of learning

Consistency: Building knowledge through small daily habits

Simplicity: Making studying feel effortless

Empowerment: Letting users define their own learning journey with personalized input

## 🌟 Vision of Success
To reach at least 100 downloads and receive positive feedback from users who feel more confident in their vocabulary or study habits — ultimately creating a small but impactful tool in someone’s daily routine.

## 🔍 What Makes It Unique
Unlike typical flashcard or memorization apps, this app doesn’t require scheduled study time. Instead, it brings the learning to you — through automatic daily notifications with user-generated content. It’s like a mental nudge from your past self, making review a seamless part of your day.
